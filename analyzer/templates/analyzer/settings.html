ℹ️ About ResumeAI
Project information and tech stack.
🤖
ResumeAI is an AI-powered resume analyzer that uses Natural Language Processing to compare your resume against job descriptions. It extracts technical skills, calculates TF-IDF cosine similarity, and provides an overall match score with detailed skill gap analysis to help you tailor your resume for any job.
ResumeAI  ·  Powered by Python · Django · NLP · scikit-learn
